About Blend Studioo

A studio built around the person in the chair.

Blend Studioo is a hair, beauty and grooming studio in Baner, Pune. The name is the brief: a blend of craft and care, of technique and warmth.

We keep the studio calm, the pace unhurried and the standards unreasonably high. It’s a simple way to work — and a rare one.
