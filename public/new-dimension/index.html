The practice

Edgy, yet attainable.Current, yet timeless.

New Dimension is a partner-led interior design studio in Nagpur. Its practice is rooted in vastu and carried by an artist's hand — the founders describe pairing modern and traditional sensibilities “with a bit of unexpected whimsy”, with space-saving simplicity at the root of impactful design.

Description adapted from the firm's published About page (newdimensionngp.com).
